About

Connective tissue between science, operations, and execution.

I am a research operations professional with 5+ years of experience building and scaling operational infrastructure in complex scientific environments. Currently, I serve as the operational backbone of four research labs at UC Berkeley's Innovative Genomics Institute, supporting approximately 30 researchers across programs funded by CZI, NSF, NIH, TED Audacious, Biohub, and LBNL.

I build the systems, partnerships, and infrastructure that let researchers stay focused on the science. That includes external collaborations, procurement, sample logistics, onboarding programs, compliance documentation, dashboards, team events, and the coordination architecture that prevents work from stalling quietly in the background.

I hold an MS in Biotechnology, a BS in Chemistry, and an MBA with Honors from Boston University, completed while working full time. I use AI tools daily as a core part of how I run operations, and I am currently completing the UC Berkeley Extension Data Science program.
